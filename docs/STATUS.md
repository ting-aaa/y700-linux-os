# Hardware and system status

Evidence through 2026-11-20 covers one TB320FC_PRC with 16 GiB RAM, 512 GB storage and CN350 Android firmware. Its existing Debian installation now has platform dev12.8. Xfce exposes hardware keyboard detection, speaker volume and three tablet display controls. The owner confirmed sustained Chromium video audio at a comfortable level from the calibrated speakers. On the tablet CPU, a private 200% Xfce desktop entered 你好 and then English in GTK and Chromium. Physical cold boot of the new combined image and touch-only first enrollment on the built-in panel remain open. The dev12.8 archive booted from EFI in ARM64 QEMU. After first-user enrollment, Xfce started one ambient-light worker with automatic brightness disabled. The installed English and Chinese windows also passed a 200% private desktop trial on the tablet CPU. The fixture supplied synthetic light and an isolated backlight. Manual 25% took over from automatic control and lock or blanking released the sensor. The physical backlight remained at 102/2047 and SLPI stayed offline. The effect of real changes in illumination on the lit display remains open. [Brightness and test](BRIGHTNESS.md#automatic-brightness) · [Dev12.8 archive](ADAPTIVE-BRIGHTNESS-IMAGE.md)

The dev12.6 archive contains the 1,115-package desktop with the touch locker and current camera and rotation packages preinstalled. Two ARM64 QEMU EFI boots used the same account. Touchscreen enrollment succeeded on the first boot. After a full power-off the second boot went straight to the graphical login. In each session, a wrong unlock password was rejected and the correct password restored the open GTK window and its unsaved text. The complete archive was separately extracted as an ordinary Linux user. Physical cold boot of this combination remains open. [Image guide](DEV12.6-IMAGE.md) · [Boot evidence and checksums](BOARD-IMAGE.md#dev126-standalone-image)

The same dev12.6 root filesystem now also runs from RAM on the tablet's existing kernel. A USB transfer took 33 seconds. The board verified the full 6 GiB image and its 1,115 installed packages. An ordinary account ran Xfce at 2560×1600 on a private virtual display with GTK at 200%. Clicking five letters on the visible Onboard keyboard entered a folder name in Thunar. After the test, the original desktop session and services remained active and the RAM staging was removed. The complete RAM system separately passed a diskless ARM64 QEMU boot, touchscreen enrollment, PAM login, and shutdown. [RAM image and tablet evidence](DEV12.6-RAM-TRIAL.md)

The dev12.7 archive adds the native Xfce battery indicator and UPower to the dev12.6 board image. An ordinary Linux user extracted the archive and verified all three images and the 1,124-package inventory. On its first ARM64 QEMU EFI boot, Xfce started the packaged power manager in the enrolled user's session and the system UPower service was active. The tablet has the same power manager update installed. An ordinary user read the real battery and USB supply through UPower. In a separate 2560×1600 session on the tablet CPU, primary and secondary clicks on the battery icon opened a menu that launched Power Manager Settings. The complete dev12.7 image still needs a physical cold boot. [Image and unpacking](DEV12.7-IMAGE.md) · [Power manager update](POWER-MANAGER-TOUCH.md)
