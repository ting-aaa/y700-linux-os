Evidence through 2026-11-20 covers one TB320FC_PRC with 16 GiB RAM, 512 GB storage and CN350 Android firmware. The tablet runs platform dev12.4, with a native speaker control on its Xfce top bar. It exposes the three display controls in Xfce's Hardware settings. Its calibrated internal speakers play Chromium videos at an owner-confirmed comfortable level. The 1,112-package ARM64 image also includes the dev10 touch-mapping and Xorg panel-size fixes. Dev9 introduced Intelligent Pinyin and a per-user English or Simplified Chinese display setting. On the tablet's CPU, a private 200% Xfce desktop entered 你好 and then English in both GTK and a sandboxed Chromium 156 web form. An attended physical boot of the entire new image and touch-only first enrollment on the built-in panel remain open.
